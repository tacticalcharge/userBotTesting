STAFF PENALTY RULES

GENERAL RULE:
- Before taking disciplinary action, staff should first advise the user.
- If the user continues the behavior after being advised, staff may proceed with the appropriate punishment.
- If staff are unsure about a situation, they should ask an appropriate staff member.
- The examples below are examples only. A violation does not need to exactly match an example to qualify.
- Always judge the actual meaning and context of the message.

PERMANENT BAN:

1. DDoS / Doxing
   Rule:
   - DDoS attacks against the server or its members -> Permanent Ban
   - Doxing attacks against the server or its members -> Permanent Ban
   - Referencing real-life information about players with the intent to harass them -> Permanent Ban

   Examples:
   - "I'm going to DDoS the server."
   - "I'll knock your internet offline."
   - "I found your IP address and I'm going to use it."
   - "I know where you live, [real address]."
   - Posting someone's real address, phone number, or other private real-life information to harass them.
   - Threatening to expose someone's private information.

2. Trading
   Rule:
   - Selling or trading products, whether internal or external to LunaBot -> Permanent Ban

   Examples:
   - "Selling my LunaBot account for $20."
   - "Anyone want to buy my account?"
   - "I'll trade you this account for a Steam key."
   - "Selling LunaBot products for real money."
   - Attempting to arrange a trade involving LunaBot-related products.

3. Impersonation
   Rule:
   - Impersonating LunaBot -> Permanent Ban
   - Impersonating a staff member -> Permanent Ban

   Examples:
   - "I am the official LunaBot developer."
   - Pretending to be a staff member in order to deceive users.
   - Using a staff member's identity and claiming to be them.
   - Claiming to have official staff authority when you do not.
   - Creating a misleading identity that makes users believe you are LunaBot.


MUTES:

1. Profanity
   Rule:
   - Using vulgar or offensive language -> 3-hour Mute

   Examples:
   - Excessive use of profanity.
   - Directly using vulgar language toward another user.
   - "Fuck you."
   - "What the fuck are you doing?"

   Note:
   - Consider context. A single mild swear word may not necessarily warrant punishment unless the server's rules indicate otherwise.

2. Insults
   Rule:
   - Insulting staff -> 3-day Mute
   - Insulting the server -> 7-day Mute

   Examples:
   Staff:
   - "You're a useless moderator."
   - "The staff here are idiots."
   - Directly insulting a moderator because of their staff position.

   Server:
   - "This server is fucking garbage."
   - "This server is trash and everyone here is pathetic."
   - Directly attacking or insulting the server/community as a whole.

3. Chat Toxicity
   Rule:
   - Toxic or provocative behavior in chat -> 1-day Mute

   Examples:
   - Repeatedly provoking another user to start an argument.
   - Deliberately escalating arguments.
   - Constantly antagonizing other users.
   - "Keep crying, nobody likes you."
   - Repeatedly attempting to start drama.

4. Flood / Message Spam
   Rule:
   - Repetitive spamming of messages -> 3-hour Mute

   Examples:
   - Sending the same message repeatedly.
   - Sending dozens of messages in rapid succession.
   - Repeatedly sending meaningless messages to fill the chat.
   - "AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA" sent repeatedly.
   - Copy-pasting the same text multiple times.

5. Death Wishes
   Rule:
   - Threats or wishes of death towards other users -> 3-day Mute

   Examples:
   - "I hope you die."
   - "Go kill yourself."
   - "You should die."
   - Threatening to kill another user.
   - Telling someone that they deserve to die.

   Note:
   - Distinguish genuine threats or targeted death wishes from clearly fictional, joking, or unrelated statements when context makes the intent clear.

6. Social Media / External Website Spam
   Rule:
   - Spamming social media platforms such as YouTube or Twitch -> 7-day Mute
   - Spamming websites that are not related to LunaBot -> 7-day Mute

   Examples:
   - Repeatedly posting a YouTube channel link.
   - Repeatedly posting Twitch streams.
   - Posting the same external website link over and over.
   - Repeatedly advertising another Discord server.
   - Flooding chat with unrelated website links.

7. Political / Religious Endorsement
   Rule:
   - Inappropriate expressions regarding political or religious acts -> 3-day Mute

   Examples:
   - Aggressively promoting a political movement when inappropriate for the server.
   - Repeatedly pushing political propaganda.
   - Inappropriately promoting or attacking a religion.
   - Using the server to repeatedly campaign for a political or religious cause.

   Note:
   - Normal discussion of politics or religion is not automatically a violation.
   - The context and whether the behavior is inappropriate for the server are important.

8. Discrimination
   Rule:
   - Racism -> 5-day Mute
   - Homophobia -> 5-day Mute
   - Insults targeting a group of people -> 5-day Mute

   Examples:
   - Using racial slurs against someone.
   - Making racist statements about a racial or ethnic group.
   - Using homophobic slurs.
   - Attacking someone because of their sexual orientation.
   - Insulting or degrading an entire protected group.

   Note:
   - Targeted discriminatory behavior should be distinguished from neutral discussion or quoting something for legitimate reasons.
9. Advertisement

Rule:
- Advertising other Discord servers -> 14-day Mute
- Advertising other bots -> 14-day Mute
- Advertising other services -> 14-day Mute

Examples:
- Advertising another Discord server.
- Advertising or recommending another Discord bot.
- Advertising an external service, community, website, or event.
- "Join my Minecraft UHC tournament, DM me if you want to join."
- "Come join our Minecraft server."
- "Check out this other Discord server."
- "Use [BotName] instead of LunaBot."
- Sending Discord invite links.
- Mentioning the name of another server and encouraging users to join it.
- Mentioning another bot and encouraging users to use it.
- Promoting an external service or community.

IMPORTANT:
- Advertising does NOT require an invite link.
- A message can be advertising even if it does not explicitly say "join my server."
- If a message promotes or attempts to recruit users for an external server, bot, service, community, or event, treat it as advertising.

LUNABOT EXCEPTION:
- Always exclude LunaBot and anything clearly associated with LunaBot from this rule.
- Mentioning, recommending, or advertising LunaBot is NOT an advertisement violation.
- Any server, bot, or service whose name contains "LunaBot" should be excluded unless the context clearly shows that it is unrelated to LunaBot.

DECISION RULE:
- Advertisement should be strict and easy to detect.
- If the message clearly promotes an external server, bot, service, community, or event, apply the 14-day mute.
- Do not punish ordinary conversations that merely mention another server, bot, service, or game without promoting it.
DECISION RULES:

- Identify the most relevant violation.
- Prefer a specific rule over a general rule.
- If multiple rules apply, select the most severe applicable punishment.
- Do not invent a rule or punishment.
- Do not assume a violation solely because a message contains a particular word.
- Consider the surrounding context when it is provided.
- If the message does not violate any rule, recommend no punishment.
- If there is not enough information to confidently determine whether a violation occurred, recommend no punishment rather than guessing.
- The examples are NOT a complete list of violations.
